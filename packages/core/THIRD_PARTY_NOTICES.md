## Changes

Imported SVGs were converted to typed vector nodes, categorized, and given consistent canvas controls. Geometry is preserved. User customization can modify colors, line widths, transforms, and canvas dimensions. SVG exports retain source and license attribution in their description.

Game Icons includes contributions from the artists listed at https://game-icons.net/about.html#authors. Source files and per-icon history are available at https://github.com/game-icons/icons.

Brand logos are the property of their respective owners. An artwork license does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement. Consult the source brand guidelines for use of a mark.
